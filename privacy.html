Privacy Policy

1. What this covers

This is a static portfolio site — it doesn't have user accounts, a backend, or a database. This policy explains the little data that does pass through it.

2. Hosting & basic logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel's infrastructure automatically logs standard technical data for every visit — things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps — for security and reliability purposes. I don't have a separate analytics tool running, and I don't personally collect or sell any of this data.

3. Cookies

This site doesn't set its own cookies or use tracking pixels. If that ever changes (for example, if analytics are added later), this page will be updated to say so.

4. Third-party links

Links to X, LinkedIn, and Behance take you to their platforms, each with their own privacy policy. Anything you do on those platforms is governed by their terms, not this one.

5. Contact information you send me

If you reach out directly (for example, via X or email) about work or a project, I'll use whatever you share only to respond to you — not for marketing lists or anything unrelated.

6. Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If how this site handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will reflect it.
